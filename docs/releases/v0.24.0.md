# v0.24.0

**Release Date:** 2026-11-11

## Highlights

- **MEL plans in `goals/mel`.** A monitoring, evaluation, and learning plan in one validated document: a results framework from outputs to impact, the activities that produce it, an indicator reference sheet per indicator, the assumptions to monitor, an evaluation plan, a learning agenda, and a review and reporting schedule. It comes with validation presets, Markdown and Pandoc renderers, and a generated JSON Schema.
- **`splan goals` works with every goal framework.** `goals validate` and `goals init` handle OKR, V2MOM, VSEM, DMAIC, and MEL, and new `goals vsem`, `goals dmaic`, and `goals mel` subcommands validate, render, and create each format.

## Overview

A MEL plan says how a team will know whether its work is producing the results
it intends, and what it will do with what it learns. Monitoring tracks
indicators continuously; evaluation asks, at set times, whether and why
results happened; learning turns both into decisions. MEL plans are common in
program work and just as useful for a product or business team: the full
example is the onboarding plan of a fictional bookkeeping app.

See the [MEL guide](../goals/mel.md).

## Changes

### goals/mel

- `Plan`, `Metadata`, `Result`, `Activity`, `Indicator`, `Baseline`, `Target`, `DataQuality`, `Assumption`, `Evaluation`, `LearningQuestion`, `Review`, and `Report`, with `New`, `ReadFile`, `Parse`, and `WriteFile`.
- Results sit at five levels (`output`, `short_term`, `medium_term`, `long_term`, `impact`) and name the results they contribute to. `TopResults`, `Contributors`, `ActivitiesFor`, and `IndicatorsFor` read the framework.
- An indicator `measures` a result, an activity whose delivery it tracks, or an assumption it watches. Its reference sheet holds the definition, type, unit, direction, disaggregation, baseline, targets, data source, collection method, frequency, owner, and data quality checks and limitations.
- Evaluations have questions, a type (`baseline`, `process`, `formative`, `midterm`, `final`, `impact`, `developmental`), a method, an evaluator, and dates. Learning questions have the decision each informs, how and by when it will be answered, and what it bears on. Reports say what each audience needs to know, what it receives, and how often.
- `Validate` with two presets. Structural problems are always errors: a missing or duplicate ID, an unknown reference or one to the wrong kind of element, a cycle in the results framework, an unknown value, or a bad date. Gaps in the collection plan, evaluation plan, learning agenda, and schedule are warnings under `DefaultValidationOptions` and errors under `StrictValidationOptions`.
- Renderers in `goals/mel/render/markdown` and `goals/mel/render/pandoc` (with front matter for a PDF). The results framework is drawn as a nested list from impact down, and a result shared by several branches is drawn once. `SummaryOptions` leaves out the reference sheets. Both produce the same bytes for the same plan.

### splan goals

- `splan goals validate FILE` validates any goal document with its framework's own rules. The framework is detected from the file's fields, or given with `--framework`, and `--strict` treats gaps as errors. It previously checked OKR files only, with a few rules of its own.
- `splan goals init --framework` creates a template for `okr`, `v2mom`, `vsem`, `dmaic`, or `mel`.
- `splan goals vsem`, `goals dmaic`, and `goals mel` each have `validate` (with `--strict`), `generate` (VSEM as `markdown` or `marp`, DMAIC as `marp` or `pandoc`, MEL as `markdown` or `pandoc` with `--summary`), and `init`. The VSEM, DMAIC, and MEL templates meet their strict options.
- In Go, `cli.ValidateGoals`, `cli.DetectGoalFramework`, and the `VSEMTemplate`, `DMAICTemplate`, and `MELTemplate` functions.

### Schema

`schema/mel.schema.json` is generated from the MEL types and embedded as
`schema.MELSchema()` and `schema.MELSchemaBytes()`. `splan schema generate
--type` accepts `vsem`, `dmaic`, and `mel` as well as `prd`, `okr`, and
`v2mom`.

### Examples

`examples/minimal.mel.json` and `examples/product.mel.json` are checked to
validate; the full example also meets the strict options.

## Compatibility

Additive for Go callers. An API comparison against v0.23.0 reports the new
`schema` and `cli` identifiers as compatible changes, and no incompatible
changes. On the command line, `splan goals validate` now applies each
framework's full rules, so an OKR file that passed its earlier checks may
report findings it did not before.
