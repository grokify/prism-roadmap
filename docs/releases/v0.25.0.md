# v0.25.0

**Release Date:** 2026-11-11

## Highlights

- **OGSM joins OKR, V2MOM, and VSEM in `goals`.** `goals/ogsm` models OGSM (Objective, Goals, Strategies, Measures) plans: one objective in words, goals in numbers that say it has been reached, the strategies that reach them, the measures that track each strategy, and the actions that carry the strategies out. It comes with validation presets, Markdown and Marp renderers, a generated JSON Schema, and a `splan goals ogsm` command.

## Overview

An OGSM puts a plan on one page, two parts in words and two in numbers. Goals
measure the objective and move slowly; measures track each strategy and move
within weeks, so they say early whether a strategy is working. OGSMs cascade:
a strategy at one level becomes the objective of the level below.

See the [OGSM guide](../goals/ogsm.md).

## Changes

### goals/ogsm

- `OGSM`, `Metadata`, `Objective`, `Goal`, `Strategy`, `Measure`, and `Action`, with `New`, `ReadFile`, `Parse`, and `WriteFile`.
- Strategies name the goals they serve; measures and actions name their strategy. `Goal`, `Strategy`, `StrategiesFor`, `MeasuresFor`, and `ActionsFor` read the plan.
- Cascading: `metadata.parentId` names the parent OGSM, and `objective.parentStrategyId` the parent strategy the objective carries out.
- `Validate` with two presets. Structural problems are always errors: no objective, goals, or strategies, a missing or duplicate ID, an unknown goal, strategy, or action, an unknown status, priority, or direction, and a bad date. Missing targets, owners, the objective's due date, measures, actions, and links between goals and strategies are warnings under `DefaultValidationOptions` and errors under `StrictValidationOptions`, which also warns at more than five goals or five strategies.
- Renderers in `goals/ogsm/render/markdown` (a document that opens with the classic one-page table: objective, goals, strategies, and measures side by side) and `goals/ogsm/render/marp` (slides). Both produce the same bytes for the same plan.

### goals

`goals.FrameworkOGSM`, `goals.NewOGSM`, and `Goals.IsOGSM` add OGSM to the
union. An OGSM's strategies are its goal items, and its goals and then its
measures its result items, labelled "Strategies" and "Goals and Measures". A
goal has no `GoalID`, since it measures the objective.

### splan

- `splan goals ogsm` has `validate` (with `--strict`), `generate markdown`, `generate marp` (with `--theme`), and `init`. The template meets the strict options, with its objective due three years from today.
- `splan goals validate` detects an OGSM from its single `objective`, and `splan goals init --framework` and `splan schema generate --type` accept `ogsm`.
- The goals command generators share one read-and-render helper.

### Schema

`schema/ogsm.schema.json` is generated from the OGSM types and embedded as
`schema.OGSMSchema()` and `schema.OGSMSchemaBytes()`. The PRD schema is
regenerated because it embeds the goals union, which now has an `ogsm` option.

### Examples

`examples/minimal.ogsm.json` and `examples/product.ogsm.json`, a fictional
bookkeeping app's three-year plan, are checked to validate. The full example
also meets the strict options.

### Documentation

- An [OGSM guide](../goals/ogsm.md) covering the four parts, cascading, how OGSM differs from VSEM and V2MOM, validation, goals alignment, rendering, and the command line.
- OGSM in the README, the [goals overview](../goals/overview.md), the documentation home, the API reference, and the getting-started pages.

## Compatibility

Additive only. An API comparison against v0.24.0 reports the new `goals`,
`schema`, and `cli` identifiers as compatible changes, and no incompatible
changes. JSON written by v0.24.0 reads unchanged.
