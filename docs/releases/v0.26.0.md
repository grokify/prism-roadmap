# v0.26.0

**Release Date:** 2026-11-12

## Highlights

- **The Balanced Scorecard joins OKR, V2MOM, VSEM, and OGSM in `goals`.** `goals/bsc` models a scorecard as strategic objectives in perspectives, linked cause to effect in a strategy map, with lead and lag measures and the initiatives that close the gaps. It comes with validation presets, Markdown and Marp renderers, a generated JSON Schema, and a `splan goals bsc` command.
- **Every goal framework's Markdown can sit inside a larger document.** `OmitHeader` leaves out a document's title, and `HeadingOffset` moves its headings down, for the VSEM, OGSM, MEL, DMAIC, and Balanced Scorecard renderers. DMAIC gains a portable Markdown renderer.

## Overview

A Balanced Scorecard describes a strategy as objectives in a few
perspectives, balanced so that the results an organization reports are not
pursued at the expense of what produces them. The four classic perspectives
are financial, customer, internal process, and learning and growth; they may be
renamed, reordered, or replaced, and a public agency or nonprofit often puts a
mission perspective on top. Lead measures say early whether the strategy is
working; lag measures say whether it worked. See the
[Balanced Scorecard guide](../goals/bsc.md).

A tool that builds one document from several plans, such as a theory of
change followed by a VSEM and a MEL plan, can now place each renderer's output
under a heading of its own. With `OmitHeader` and a `HeadingOffset` of 1, a
plan's level-two sections become level three, under the caller's level-two
heading. See
[Embedding rendered plans](../goals/overview.md#embedding-rendered-plans).

## Changes

### goals/bsc

- `BSC`, `Metadata`, `Perspective`, `Theme`, `Objective`, `Measure`, `Thresholds`, and `Initiative`, with `New`, `ReadFile`, `Parse`, and `WriteFile`. `New` and `DefaultPerspectives` give the four classic perspectives, top first; each perspective's `kind` says which classic role it plays, whatever it is called.
- Objectives name their perspective and their strategic themes, and link cause to effect with `contributesTo`. Measures name their objective, are `lead` or `lag`, and carry a baseline, a target, and `thresholds` at which they turn green or red. Initiatives name the objectives they serve. `ObjectivesIn`, `ObjectivesInTheme`, `ContributorsTo`, `MeasuresFor`, and `InitiativesFor` read the scorecard.
- Cascading: `metadata.parentId` names the scorecard a team's scorecard cascades from.
- `Validate` with two presets. Structural problems are always errors, including a cycle in the strategy map; a link that runs down the perspectives is a warning. Missing targets, owners, measure types, measures, strategy-map links, and a lead or lag measure on each objective are warnings under `DefaultValidationOptions` and errors under `StrictValidationOptions`.
- Renderers in `goals/bsc/render/markdown` (with the strategy map as a Mermaid diagram) and `goals/bsc/render/marp` (slides, with the strategy map as a table). Both produce the same bytes for the same scorecard.

### goals

`goals.FrameworkBSC`, `goals.NewBSC`, and `Goals.IsBSC` add the Balanced
Scorecard to the union. A scorecard's objectives are its goal items, tagged
with their perspective, and its measures its result items, labelled
"Objectives" and "Measures".

### Embedding the Markdown renderers

- `OmitHeader` and `HeadingOffset` on the render `Options` of `goals/vsem`, `goals/ogsm`, `goals/mel`, `goals/dmaic`, and `goals/bsc`.
- `OmitHeader` leaves out the title and the metadata line under it. `HeadingOffset` pushes every heading down that many levels, capped at level 6, and leaves fenced code blocks, such as Mermaid diagrams, alone.
- Only the Markdown renderers read them; the Marp and Pandoc renderers ignore them. Both default to off, and the output with the defaults is unchanged.

### goals/dmaic

`goals/dmaic/render/markdown` writes a DMAIC document as portable Markdown,
with no front matter, LaTeX, or page breaks: an overview with health by
category, a metrics table for each category, and detail for each metric that
needs attention. The phase distribution, root causes, initiatives, and process
capability metrics follow when their options are on. The output is
deterministic.

### splan

- `splan goals bsc` has `validate` (with `--strict`), `generate markdown`, `generate marp` (with `--theme`), and `init`. The template meets the strict options.
- `splan goals validate` detects a scorecard from its `perspectives`, and `splan goals init --framework` and `splan schema generate --type` accept `bsc`.
- `splan goals dmaic generate markdown` writes the new DMAIC format, beside `marp` and `pandoc`.

### Schema

`schema/bsc.schema.json` is generated from the Balanced Scorecard types and
embedded as `schema.BSCSchema()` and `schema.BSCSchemaBytes()`. The PRD schema
is regenerated because it embeds the goals union, which now has a `bsc`
option.

### Examples

`examples/minimal.bsc.json` and `examples/product.bsc.json`, a fictional
bookkeeping app's scorecard with two strategic themes, are checked to
validate. The full example also meets the strict options.

## Compatibility

Additive only. An API comparison against v0.25.0 reports the new `goals`,
`schema`, and `cli` identifiers, the new render options, and the new packages
as compatible changes, and no incompatible changes. JSON written by v0.25.0
reads unchanged, and Markdown output with the default options is
byte-identical to v0.25.0.
